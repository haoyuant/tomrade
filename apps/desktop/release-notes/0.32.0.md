## en
- Sign in to your Tomrade account with a passkey, and add one under Settings → Account, the new tab for your account.
- Account emails are written in the language the app runs in, and their links open from mainland China as well.
- Tell the assistant a goal and it starts an auto run with the tickets it depends on; change the goal and the plan changes with it. Auto runs go in terminal panes unless you ask for sessions, and wait on a ticket that turned to you instead of skipping what depends on it.
- A merge conflict goes back to its session by itself; what Tomrade cannot settle waits on you, plainly marked.
- A task sent to a session still mid-turn goes once the turn ends, instead of being lost.
- A new terminal takes an empty cell first, otherwise splits the largest one.
- The Inbox count is an unread badge. Comments or closes on GitHub that fail are shown on the entry instead of retried silently, and an issue split into several tickets is closed only once all of them ship.
- Settings → Worktrees can also remove finished tickets' worktrees made outside the Hub. A terminal left open no longer keeps a worktree "in use", and the worktree lists have a Check again button.
- The `tomrade create` command takes `--work-type coding|general`.

## zh
- 可以用通行密钥（passkey）登录 Tomrade 账号，在新的 设置 → 账号 页里添加。
- 账号邮件用 App 当前的语言写，邮件里的链接在中国大陆也能打开。
- 对助手说出目标，它会连同依赖的工单一起发起自动执行；目标变了，计划跟着改。自动执行默认在终端格子里跑；工单转到你手上时会等你，不再跳过依赖它的工单。
- 合并冲突会自动交回给对应的会话；Tomrade 解决不了的会明显标出来，等你处理。
- 发给仍在本轮中的会话的任务，会在本轮结束后送达，不再丢失。
- 新开的终端先用空格子，没有空格子就拆分最大的那个。
- 收件箱的数量改成未读徽标。发往 GitHub 的评论或关闭失败时会显示在条目上，不再静默重试；一个 issue 拆成多张工单时，要等全部上线才关闭。
- 设置 → Worktree 可以选择同时自动移除在 Hub 之外建的已结束工单的 worktree。开着的终端格子不会再让 worktree 一直显示“还有会话在里面”，worktree 列表多了“重新检查”按钮。
- `tomrade create` 命令支持 `--work-type coding|general`。
