Agent access

What your coding agents may do to this backlog, and what they may not.

MCP · listening

http://127.0.0.1:19090/mcp

Writes arrive with the baseVersion the agent read. A stale version is rejected, never merged.

Human work is protected
  • Locked fields reject agent writes with a typed error
  • Every write is recorded in the oplog with its author
  • ⌘Z reverts any write, agent or human
